Hi! I'm Anish Kumar Patel,a BSc (Hons) Cybersecurity & Digital Forensics student at The British College, Kathmandu. I'm building my foundation in cybersecurity through hands-on learning, practical projects, networking labs, and Capture The Flag challenges.
